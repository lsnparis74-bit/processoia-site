Who is responsible?

Louison (ProcessoIA), Lisbon, Portugal. For any question: contact@processoia.com

Audience measurement

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. This tool sets no cookies and creates no personal identifier: it provides overall statistics (pages viewed, country, device type, referring site).

Bookings

Calls are booked through Cal.com (Cal.com, Inc.). The information you enter there (name, email, answers) is only used to prepare and hold the call. The Cal.com module shown on the website may set its own technical cookies; its privacy policy applies.

Emails

Messages sent to contact@processoia.com are only used to reply to you and follow up on our conversation.

Cookies

ProcessoIA sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The website only remembers, in your browser, the language you chose.

Technical services

The website is hosted by Netlify, which keeps technical logs (IP address, date, page requested) for security and proper operation. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and some libraries from jsDelivr and unpkg: these services receive your IP address when they load.

Retention

Exchanges with prospects are kept for at most 3 years after the last contact; exchanges with clients for the duration of the relationship and the legal retention periods for accounting documents.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, or object to its processing, by writing to contact@processoia.com. You can also contact the data protection authority: the CNPD in Portugal or the CNIL in France.
