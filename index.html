    Something happens: a form is filled, an email lands, a date arrives.

  2. 02

    Process

  3. 03

06Why AI changes everything

From rigid rules to real judgment

Classic automation follows fixed steps. AI adds an understanding of language and context: it reads an email, understands a request and picks the right action. An AI agent goes further and carries a task through from start to finish, over several steps.

Before

  • Hours of manual, repetitive work
  • Rigid rules that break on edge cases
  • Business hours only
  • Slow replies to customers and leads

With AI

  • Minutes instead of hours
  • Judgment that adapts to nuance
  • Working 24/7, while you sleep
  • Instant, consistent responses

08Your data

Your tools, your data, your automation

Handing your emails or your clients to an automation takes trust. Here are my rules, in line with the GDPR.

Hosted in Europe

Your data stays in your own tools (Google, Microsoft…). Your automations run on a dedicated n8n instance, kept separate from other clients, on my server in Europe. A GDPR data processing agreement covers everything.

Limited access

I only access what the project needs, and you can remove my access at any time.

Professional AI

I use AI models through their professional offerings (API), whose data is not used to train the models by default.

You own it

Everything built belongs to you: the automations, their documentation and the access. It is your property: you can view them, have them changed or deleted whenever you want, and take them with you to run elsewhere.
